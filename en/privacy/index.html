Privacy

Clarity about your data, too.

These notes describe the technical state of the local preview. They are not an approved privacy notice for a public hotel website.

No tracking or external embeds

This preview does not include analytics or marketing services. Fonts, photographs, scripts and illustrations are served locally. No non-essential cookies are set, and no contact details are stored in localStorage.

For that reason, there is no decorative cookie banner. If a booking provider, tracking or other services are added later, the legal basis, technical consent controls and notices must be reviewed accordingly.

The enquiry assistant

Your entries are processed only in your browser’s working memory. This preview has no enquiry server endpoint, no database and no automatic email sending.

You can open an email draft, copy the text or download it as a file. The selected email service receives the data only when you send it yourself. That service’s own privacy information also applies. Do not enter health information, identity document copies or payment details in the message field.

External links

Maps, hospital pages and transport websites are not embedded. They open only when you choose a link. You then leave this website; the relevant provider’s privacy information applies to its processing.

To be completed before public operation

The controller, hosting, server logs and retention periods, data processing arrangements, recipients, any international transfers and the exercise of data subject rights must be reviewed and fully documented for the actual operating environment.

The hotel contact shown in this preview comes from the existing website. It is not evidence that the hotel is the controller of this independent local preview. For current hotel information, please visit the existing official website.
