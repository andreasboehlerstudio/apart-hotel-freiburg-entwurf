Terms & conditions

Discuss the details. Plan with confidence.

An enquiry draft is not a reservation. Availability, full prices and the terms for your stay are provided directly by the hotel.

What this website does

The assistant helps you put together a non-binding enquiry draft with your preferred room, travel dates and extras. It does not check live availability or reserve a room.

A summary, an opened email draft or a downloaded text file is not a booking confirmation.

Please confirm the current terms

Existing online information about arrival, departure and cancellation is inconsistent. This preview therefore does not present any one version as binding.

Ask the hotel to confirm the check-in and check-out times, cancellation terms, total costs and extras for your dates in your personal offer. No general free cancellation or best-price guarantee is promised.

Contracts and other booking methods

Any contract depends on the specific agreement with the hotel. The existing hotel terms also provide for telephone bookings with verbal confirmation. This page therefore does not suggest that bookings can become binding only in writing.

This page is not a new, legally reviewed version of the hotel’s terms and conditions. The operator and its legal advisers must approve a consistent, up-to-date version before public use.
