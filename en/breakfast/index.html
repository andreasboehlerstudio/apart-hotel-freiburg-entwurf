01 / A first impression

Time for breakfast.

Breakfast is an optional extra. Add it as a request in your enquiry. We will confirm the current price and availability with your offer.

  • Monday to Friday: 07:00–10:00
  • Weekends and public holidays: 07:30–10:00
  • Breakfast is not automatically included with your room
