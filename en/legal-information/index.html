Transparency about original photography, the new design and the technical state of this website proposal.

Photography and proposed visual identity

The hotel photographs come from the existing publicly available hotel website. They show the actual property and should be understood as room examples. No photographs of unrelated luxury hotels or generatively renovated rooms have been used to represent real facilities.

Public access to an image file is not a licence to use it. These files are used here for an internal design preview. Rights holders, licence scope, required credits and any model releases must be clarified before publication.

The revised wordmark, colours and landscape illustration are design proposals, not a visual identity already approved by the hotel.

External information

External links to transport, hospital and tourism services support your planning. Check current opening hours, fares and connections with the relevant provider. No general exclusion of liability or legal guarantee follows from these links.

Not a public booking service

The preview is marked to discourage normal search engine indexing. Approvals, hosting configuration and a tested enquiry or booking integration are needed before it goes live. A noindex instruction is not access protection.

The full technical and editorial approval requirements are recorded in the project documentation. No changes have been made to the existing hotel website.
