If you are coming to Freiburg for treatment or accompanying someone close to you, it helps to have a place to retreat to. Our hotel is directly opposite the university hospital complex.

01 / HOTEL MOMENTS. PERSONALLY YOURS.
01 / A first impression

For treatment, visits and company.

Apart Hotel is a small hotel with 17 rooms, all with their own balcony. Whether you are travelling alone or staying as a couple, we will check your requested dates personally.

A little more detail

The university hospital has several buildings and locations. Please check the exact address of the clinic you need. Being opposite the hospital complex does not imply a fixed walking time to every department.

Check locations on the official hospital website (external website, new window)
02 / HOTEL MOMENTS. PERSONALLY YOURS.
02 / THE LITTLE THINGS.

A few things to make your stay easier.

Free Wi-Fi, a fridge, a kettle and the option to add breakfast can help you plan your day. Special terms for longer stays can be requested.

  • Discuss room choice and occupancy with us
  • Request parking in advance
  • Clarify specific access requirements by telephone
  • Discuss a possible extension if your dates are uncertain
A little more detail

Please tell us about practical requirements for your room or arrival. Do not include medical diagnoses, test results or hospital documents in the open enquiry form.

03 / GOOD TO KNOW.

A personal conversation. No false promises.

Our hotel provides accommodation; it does not provide medical care. We cannot make general guarantees of accessible rooms, special care services or flexible cancellation.

A little more detail

Send an enquiry or call us. We will discuss what we can offer for your particular visit.
