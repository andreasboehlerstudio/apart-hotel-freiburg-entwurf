01 / HOTEL MOMENTS. PERSONALLY YOURS.
01 / A first impression

By car. With a reserved parking space.

Parking spaces and garages are located directly at the hotel. A reservation is required and charges apply. Please include your parking request with your enquiry.

A little more detail

We will confirm the current charge and how it is calculated in your personal offer. A power socket in the garage is not the same as a dedicated EV wallbox or rapid charging point.

03 / GOOD TO KNOW.

A few things before you arrive.

Please confirm current check-in and check-out times directly with the hotel, especially if you expect to arrive late or are planning a longer stay.

A little more detail

According to the hotel description, the rooms can be reached by lift. If you need step-free access, specific room features or other practical arrangements, please call before booking. General physical accessibility has not been confirmed.
