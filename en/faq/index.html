Small questions.
Clear answers.

From room amenities to making an enquiry: find answers to help you plan. If anything is still unclear, we would rather talk to you directly.

Rooms & amenities

Does every room really have a balcony?+

Yes. All 17 rooms at Apart Hotel Freiburg have their own balcony. Views differ from room to room. The photographs on this website show example rooms.

Is there a kitchen in the room?+

According to the hotel description, a fridge and kettle are included. A kitchen or kitchenette is not listed. If you have particular requirements for a longer stay, please discuss them with the hotel in advance.

What room categories are available?+

You can request single rooms, small double rooms, large double rooms and family rooms for three or four guests. Single and small double rooms have a shower; large double rooms and family rooms have a bathtub.

Is Wi-Fi free?+

Yes. Free Wi-Fi is listed among the room amenities, as are a desk, TV, telephone, fridge and kettle.

Arrival & your stay

What are the check-in and check-out times?+

Please confirm current arrival and departure times directly with the hotel and refer to your personal offer. Existing online information is inconsistent; the times for this website must be agreed with the hotel before publication.

Can I reserve a parking space?+

Parking spaces and garages are located directly at the hotel and charges apply. A reservation is required. Please request parking together with your stay; availability and current terms will be confirmed personally.

Are pets welcome?+

Yes, pets are welcome according to the hotel description. Please mention your pet in your enquiry and ask the hotel to confirm any fees and conditions.

Is the hotel accessible?+

According to the hotel description, rooms can be reached by lift. This alone does not establish that the entrance is step-free or that the rooms are wheelchair-accessible. Please discuss your specific access requirements with the hotel by telephone before arrival.

Breakfast & services

When is breakfast served?+

The hotel description lists the breakfast buffet from 07:00 to 10:00 Monday to Friday and from 07:30 to 10:00 on weekends and public holidays. Breakfast is an optional extra; the price will be provided with your offer.

Are vegetarian or vegan breakfast options available?+

The hotel says it takes vegetarian and vegan preferences into account when selecting the buffet. Please mention allergies and food intolerances before arrival. Allergen-free preparation is not guaranteed.

Can guests who are not staying at the hotel join us for breakfast?+

Yes, with a reservation. Please contact the hotel at least one day beforehand to reserve for your breakfast guests and agree availability and price.

Where can I leave my bicycle?+

Bicycle parking is offered free of charge. Charges may apply for using power sockets in the garage. A dedicated EV wallbox or rapid charging station is not guaranteed.

Enquiries & booking

Can I make a confirmed booking here?+

This preview only creates a non-binding enquiry draft. It does not show live availability or reserve a room. Availability, the total price and contractual terms are provided directly by the hotel.

How is my enquiry sent?+

The enquiry assistant prepares an email for you to review and send yourself in your own email application. You can also copy or download the text. Nothing is sent automatically, and your contact details are not stored on a server.

Are special terms available for longer stays?+

Yes. The hotel advertises special terms for longer stays. These depend on your dates, occupancy and room choice and are offered individually. No fixed discount or particular minimum stay is guaranteed here.

What cancellation terms apply?+

Existing online information differs. Please ask the hotel to confirm the terms for your stay in your personal offer. This preview does not promise free cancellation as a general rule.
