EASY TO COMPARE.

The differences
at a glance.

Single and small double rooms have a bathroom with a shower. Large double rooms add a seating area and bathtub. Family rooms for three or four guests have a double bed, a sofa bed and a bathtub.

The photographs show examples. Furnishings, layouts and views may vary. Please mention any particular requirements in your enquiry — a specific room is confirmed only by personal arrangement.

We deliberately do not show unconfirmed “from” prices. You will receive an offer for your dates, occupancy and requested extras.
